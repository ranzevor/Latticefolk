# Source-grounded environment presentation

This contract covers source-grounded tree, character-support, collision, crop and streamed-water presentation plus sourced coarse-world markers. It does not cover scene-wide primitive cleanup, all character poses, terrain alignment/seams, complete coarse-distance presentation or broader user journeys. Current-source native browser execution and actual image review remain required; source geometry tests and the read-only diagnostics below cannot establish visibility by themselves.

## Tree scale, anchor and interaction

The previous common 3.5–3.8 m height fit compressed the Cube World tree crowns to first-person eye level. It also centered the complete, asymmetric canopy bounds on the semantic anchor, rather than the trunk. `treePresentation.ts` fits the unchanged licensed source at adult reference heights of 7.0/7.4/8.4 m for tree1/tree2/tree3. Existing generated height draws remain bounded visual variation; the generator, RNG consumption, entity IDs and saved positions do not change.

The fit rotates the source before centering its actual basal vertices on the semantic anchor. The resolved basal footprint, not the full canopy bounds, is registered with the existing deterministic physics authority. A nonblocking 0.65 m exterior trigger margin surrounds that footprint. Normal first-person ray targeting, overlap validation and the existing swept tool-contact query still gate chopping. Decoration trees use the same fitting rule and basal collision, with no new decorative model or placeholder. Streamed trees also pass through this calibration instead of retaining the old small-height asset-override path; unloading removes their chunk-scoped physics as before.

`tests/environment-presentation.test.ts` loads all three real source glTFs, verifies unchanged vertex buffers, rotated basal centering, the original tree-approach lane, physical blocking/contact, reachable triggers and chunk teardown. `e2e/environment-presentation.spec.ts` uses a legal pre-boot save fixture and normal console pause, native Start/W/E/menu input, acknowledged chopping inventory and ordinary reload. Approach/contact/reload screenshots and the actual registered collider/trigger are evidence for that controlled scenario, not a complete exploration journey. The original cart/God/tree smoke route remains unchanged.

## Current skinned soles, not container Y

NPC containers still take their ground height from `FinePhysicsAuthority`. `CharacterSoles` caches the original foot-weighted source vertex indices once. After each mixer update it refreshes ancestor, mesh and skin transforms, measures the deformed lowest sole in world coordinates, and adjusts only the visual model's local translation to the authoritative ground. It replaces the old 120 ms full-body bounding-box correction and its 0.35 m discard threshold. The character's X/Z, physics body, path, inventory, provider decisions and persistence schema are not rewritten by visual support.

The four sourced character models are tested through full Idle, Walk, Run, Wave, Yes and Punch clips plus crossfades at a translated, rotated, elevated parent. Independent full-geometry sampling checks the rendered minimum after correction, not a value copied from the requested ground height. Runtime `data-character-soles` records source probe counts, animation names, measured sole heights and ground heights. Browser evidence includes an ordinary decision-paused scene (active paths and animations can still advance) and sampled moving simulation poses. These are support/grounding contracts, not foot IK, sliding-free gait, sitting, sleeping or acceptance of every possible animation. Support heights alone do not establish grounded appearance. Contact shadows and player/head clearance require separate validation.

The disposable block-character constructor and unused invisible player-avatar meshes have been removed. God View remains an observer with no player body; selection/path overlays are UI aids, not player entities or formal scene models.

## Crop surface and dielectric materials

The two vendored Kenney crop GLBs declare all four soil/stalk/grain materials with `metallicFactor = 1`. This is a source-confirmed material problem, separate from shadow-map precision or weather lighting. Unchanged upstream bytes are now retained as `*.source.glb`; `assets:prepare` checks their SHA-256 and changes only those four factors to zero. Vertex/index/normal/UV binary data, source colors, node transforms and other material properties stay unchanged. No recolor, substitute model, new texture, global material override or shadow disabling is involved. See [asset preparation](asset-preparation.md) and the [asset manifest](../THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md).

Crops stand on the actual 0.12 m soil-model top rather than starting at 0.08 m inside it. The existing native harvest/persistence case continues to provide the farm close-up/result evidence. Loader/material/tamper/idempotence regressions supplement that browser evidence; they do not establish that all weather-dependent or distant shadow artifacts are solved.

## Contact shadows and player/head clearance

The former sun retained Three.js's default small shadow-camera volume, even though characters and buildings span a much larger town. Correct sole heights outside that volume consequently had no cast ground shadow. `SunShadowView` keeps the existing sun direction and lighting strengths but follows the current player/observer focus with one fixed 2048-square shadow map. First-person half-extent is 24 m; God View uses a distance-quantized 32–96 m half-extent. Light-space texel snapping stabilizes subpixel movement, with a bounded depth interval and depth/normal biases proportional to the actual world-space shadow texel. This avoids the diagonal self-shadow stripes reproduced on double-sided source faces when the normal bias was smaller than a texel. No fake shadow mesh, new model, global material override or disabled shadow is introduced. The view adapter consumes only camera/target coordinates; it never calls world discovery, generation or materialization. God View may move this render-only volume without creating a player body or changing saved player coordinates.

The original 0.32 m locomotion body fails to cover the source heads/hair, but replacing it with an all-clip radial maximum blocked the unchanged home-oven lane. `world/characterContact.ts` now defines asymmetric source-local eye-height rectangles for the four normalized rigs. All six supported clips are sampled at 90 poses against the actual deformed vertices. `playerHeadClearance` transforms the relative player position into the current NPC heading and computes exact signed disc/rectangle separation, including rounded corners. The frontal `minimumPlayerSeparation` diagnostic is **not** a radial collider; `headYaw`, `headEnvelope` and the signed `playerHeadClearance` expose the actual oriented contract.

`FinePhysicsAuthority.moveKinematic` accepts pure optional motion constraints alongside its existing body contacts. Moving the player or moving the NPC evaluates the same pair geometry. Overlapping legacy saves can separate through ordinary input, but cannot move deeper; an NPC also cannot turn its wider side into the player at its current position. Static traversal, NPC/NPC circles, tools, cart contacts, wildlife and segment queries retain their original shapes. No source mesh, saved coordinate or provider outcome is rewritten by this repair. God View passes no player constraint. Unknown future character assets require calibration rather than silently inheriting an unrelated envelope.

Player-directed `talk`/`visit` still stops at the existing 2.2 m conversation reach. Ground-relative, viewport-clamped dialogue anchors preserve the actual server-returned text, memory, persistence and stale-response guards. This is not NPC/NPC head clearance, foot IK, sliding-free gait or sitting/sleeping acceptance. The bounded shadow window and PCFSoft filter remain enabled.

The added native-input scenario supplies only bounded idle decision responses to isolate collision; it does not remove actors, alter runtime coordinates or invoke game actions. It walks into the unchanged baker, verifies physical separation and actual dialogue through the server, waits for an acknowledged save, pans God View without discovery/player-body changes and reloads the saved position/dialogue. Current-pose, tree, crop and observer screenshots must be opened separately: matrix coverage and radius contracts are not pixel acceptance.

## Building anchors through the contact round trip

The native contact/reload case exposed a separate runtime bug: center-town building state stores the front-door interaction point, but generic object restore assigned that point to the visible model's center. After reload the house moved forward into the camera/NPC while its static body remained behind. Home buildings now use the same `restoreBuildingForLayout` rule already used by streamed buildings; current model/collider/trigger anchors remain fixed, saved storage and identity survive, and no entity is removed or repositioned to make the test pass. The native case compares all twelve actual home centers/frontages/collider/trigger records before and after reload, then reacquires the same NPC with ordinary input. See [persistence](persistence.md#building-frontage-is-not-its-body-center).

A separate bounded talk-proposal case verifies that the NPC can walk to a stationary first-person player, finish outside the source head envelope at the existing conversation reach, return an actual server dialogue and persist it. These are controlled fixtures, not full free-play or all-dialogue acceptance.

## Remaining scope

Generic dropped-item boxes, procedural wildlife visuals, terrain/roads and diagnostic coarse/debug shapes remain distinct paths that still require their own presentation decisions. Streamed water surfaces and normal settlement/wilderness coarse markers already use the sourced assets documented below; they are not remaining primitive-replacement items, but their visibility, scale, terrain alignment/seams, lifecycle and God View/materialization behavior still require separate current-source browser and image acceptance. The eight furniture targets, ovens and sourced tree/character replacements likewise do not establish whole-scene primitive elimination. Remaining models require semantically appropriate licensed sources rather than hidden targets or unrelated props. Complete terrain joins and discovered-only coarse-distance representation, NPC/NPC full-body clearance, seated/sleeping poses and the full HUD/i18n/gameplay/exploration/save/restart/performance/free-play journeys remain separate product acceptance scopes.


## Reproducible local validation

`npm run validate:local` prepares assets, saves a source manifest and complete source snapshot before the long tests, runs the full check and browser suite without retries, and keeps the executed JSON/HTML/trace evidence. A changed source set invalidates the result. Playwright uses a dedicated `data/latticefolk.e2e.sqlite`, never the regular player's save. See [local validation](local-validation.md).

## Moving-NPC yielding at actual player contact

The source-oriented head envelope exposed a real motor deadlock on the unchanged home-oven approach: Ren, already walking to a task, stepped laterally into the player's lane and both valid collision queries then stayed blocked. `world/npcYield.ts` now supplies a deterministic lateral courtesy maneuver only for NPCs with an active path that block an attempted first-person move. It projects the existing oriented head corners against that input direction, chooses the nearer clear side and advances at the existing 1.65 m/s speed through `FinePhysicsAuthority`, including every static/body/head constraint. It retains its facing while side-stepping so a turn cannot sweep hair through the player. If both sides are obstructed it remains blocked. The transient plan is discarded when input stops/reverses, the path finishes, or the player is absent in God View.

This does not move idle NPCs, teleport anyone, replace the task or destination, invoke a provider, or perform production/inventory updates. Stationary contact/dialogue assertions remain required. The oven regression retains its original native player route, collision face and ingredient/save checks. This is bounded player/moving-NPC motor avoidance, not general NPC/NPC navigation or complete crowded-scene acceptance.

## Reachable baker stance and preserved facing

A second reproduced oven obstruction was stationary crafting, not a moving-NPC deadlock. Grid planning could not reach the oven's solid semantic center, yet the generic 2.3 m outcome check allowed the baker to craft from the original front aisle. For `work`/`craft` at a baking oven, `npcWorkApproach.ts` now derives an exterior side/front stance from the resolved source collider and the rig's frontal extent. Static authority must accept both the precise stance and its grid cell. The right side is preferred deterministically, the left is considered only when statically blocked, and no valid stance means no work outcome. The original entity ID/anchor, NPC `workAt`, recipes and ingredients do not change. The actor uses ordinary pathfinding and physical movement, reaches the precise final point, and faces the oven before production. The old generic 1.55 m collision shortcut cannot authorize an oven task early. Explicit idle decisions do not cause stance movement.

Because this oriented physical boundary depends on facing, NPC snapshots now carry optional finite `heading`. Legacy version-1 snapshots without the field retain their original +Z default; new home and streamed NPC restores retain the actual heading. The additive field needs no SQLite table migration or world reset. Without it, reloading a side-facing baker could turn the wide hair back into the player's saved position. `e2e/npc-baking-stance.spec.ts` selects one bounded craft intention, verifies real movement/ingredient consumption, walks the unchanged player aisle, and checks restored position/facing/clearance. It is controlled acceptance, not unscripted play.


## Sourced streamed water patches

Streamed semantic `water_patch` objects retain their deterministic IDs, world positions, interaction capabilities, resource amount/capacity and seasonal/rain regeneration. Presentation now uses Kenney Nature Kit `ground_riverOpen.glb` from the same pinned CC0 mirror already used for crop provenance. The GLB is vendored byte-for-byte; no procedural visible water mesh is constructed. Runtime fitting scales only the authored flat surface's X/Z footprint to the existing 3.4 m water-patch footprint and places it 1.8 cm above terrain to avoid z-fighting. It adds no static collider, changes no trigger radius, and does not affect God View discovery/materialization semantics.


### Sourced streamed water surface

Semantic `water_patch` entities use the pinned Kenney Nature Kit `ground_riverOpen` surface rather than a generated cylinder. The upstream GLB is retained unchanged as `ground_riverOpen.source.glb`; the served copy changes only its reviewed `water` material `metallicFactor` from `1` to `0` through the same hash-pinned offline preparation used for Kenney crops. Geometry, pale-blue base color, roughness, semantic footprint, interaction trigger, deterministic resource state and save behavior are unchanged. This prevents the upstream fully-metallic material from rendering as a black surface in Latticefolk's non-IBL scene while keeping the visible model sourced and reproducible.


## Sourced coarse markers

Normal coarse-world markers no longer create generated house boxes or tree cones. Settlement summaries reuse the existing licensed Quaternius Ultimate Fantasy RTS town-center asset; wilderness summaries reuse the existing Cube World tree, bush or rock assets selected by biome. These are presentation-only clones: they do not add WorldObjects, collision, resources, population or discovery. If a licensed template is unavailable the marker stays empty rather than falling back to a generated visible primitive.

The coarse ground tile, center-town-scale streamed-chunk alignment and seams have an independent terrain/coarse-distance technical acceptance boundary; sourced markers do not establish those outcomes. Materializing a chunk hides its coarse marker while the authoritative fine scene is active. God View may observe already-discovered coarse markers but camera movement never calls `ensureWindowAround` and therefore cannot discover or materialize new chunks.
